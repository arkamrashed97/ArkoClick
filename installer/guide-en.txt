ArkoClick is installed.

How to use it in 3 steps:

1) Open ArkoClick from the desktop or the Start menu.

2) In the "Simple clicker" tab press "Choose the spot on screen", then click the place you want ArkoClick to click.

3) Choose a speed and press "Start". To stop, press "Stop" or the F6 key.

Good to know:
- Keep using your computer: ArkoClick never moves your mouse.
- It keeps working even if the window is minimized or behind other windows.
- The "Keyboard" tab presses a key or types a text automatically.
- The "Classic clicker" tab clicks wherever your mouse is: give it a shortcut (for example F7) and press it to start or stop.
- Change the language or switch to night mode with the buttons at the top.
- F8 stops everything immediately.

To try it: open "ArkoClick test page" from the Start menu, choose the green circle as the spot, and watch the counter.
